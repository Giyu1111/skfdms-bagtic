Frequently Asked Questions
About SK funds, DILG policies, and the Full Disclosure System
What is the DILG Full Disclosure Policy (FDP)?+
The DILG Full Disclosure Policy requires all local government units, including Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) councils, to proactively disclose their financial and accountability documents online and in conspicuous public places within prescribed timelines. This includes budgets, expenditure reports, audit reports, and project accomplishment reports.
How much is the SK budget for Barangay Bagtic?+
The SK annual budget comes from 10% of the barangay's General Fund allocation plus other sources. For FY 2025, the SK Barangay Bagtic total fund is ₱182,500. All expenditures are documented and made available here in the Transparency Dashboard.
What are the required documents under the Full Disclosure Policy?+
Required SK documents include: Annual Investment Plan (AIP), Approved Budget (ABUD), Statement of Receipts and Expenditures (SRE), SK Resolutions (RES), Minutes of Meetings (MOM), Project Accomplishment Reports (PAR), Activity/Event Reports (ACT), Supplemental Budget (SUP), and Undisbursed AIP funds (UDAI).
How can I download official SK documents?+
All published documents are freely available on the Documents page. Navigate to the Documents section, use the filters to find the document you need, and click the Download button. No registration or login is required for public access.
Who can be a member of the Sangguniang Kabataan?+
The SK is composed of Filipino citizens who are at least 18 but not more than 24 years of age on the day of election, registered voters of the katipunan ng kabataan of the barangay, and residents of the barangay for at least 6 months before the election.
How do I report a concern about SK fund usage?+
You can submit concerns through the Feedback tab on this page, or contact the SK officials directly through the Contact page. Concerns can also be raised during SK General Assembly meetings, which are open to all barangay residents.
When are SK documents required to be published?+
Under the DILG FDP, most documents must be posted within 5 days of approval or completion. Quarterly financial reports (SRE) must be posted within 20 days after each quarter ends. Annual Investment Plans must be posted within 5 days of enactment.
Can I attend SK meetings?+
SK Regular Sessions are generally open to the public and all barangay residents are encouraged to attend and participate. Special sessions may be open or closed depending on the agenda. Check the Events Calendar for scheduled meeting dates.
