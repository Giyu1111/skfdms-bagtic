About FDTMS-SK

The Full Disclosure Transparency Management System of Catigbian, Bohol — promoting transparency, accountability, and youth empowerment in local governance.

What is FDTMS-SK?

The FDTMS-SK is a web-based platform designed for the Municipality of Catigbian, Bohol. It allows SK officials to upload, organize, and publish their official documents online, making them freely accessible to the youth and entire community — anytime, anywhere.

The system was developed in response to the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) Full Disclosure Policy, which requires all SK units to proactively disclose their financial statements, resolutions, and project reports to the public.

Why It Matters

Online Access

No need to visit the barangay hall. All SK documents are available online 24/7.

Data Integrity

Documents are securely stored and cannot be altered after submission.

DILG Compliant

Covers all DILG-required disclosure categories for SK councils.
