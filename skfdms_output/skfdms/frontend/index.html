About SK-FDMS

The Sangguniang Kabataan Full Disclosure Management System of Catigbian, Bohol promotes transparent, accountable, and accessible youth governance.

Open full page ->

What is SK-FDMS?

SK-FDMS is a web-based platform that allows SK officials to upload, organize, publish, and maintain official documents online so residents can access disclosure records anytime.

The system supports the DILG Full Disclosure Policy by making budgets, reports, resolutions, meeting minutes, and project documents easier to find.

Online Access

No need to visit the barangay hall. Published SK documents are available online.

Data Integrity

Documents are stored centrally and can be reviewed through the public portal.

DILG Compliant

Covers disclosure categories expected from SK councils and local transparency efforts.
